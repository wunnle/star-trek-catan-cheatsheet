Crew Manifest

English reference for the German crew cards. Match your card by the character's name.
One on duty

You always hold exactly one crew card, face up in front of you.

Two uses

Each card can be used twice. Flip it after the first use.

Change of watch

After the second use, return it and pick any other available crew card.
