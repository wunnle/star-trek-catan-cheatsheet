Finding a card

Match the full name on the top plate and the letter badge on the portrait.

A5
James T. Kirk
Schutz vor der 7Protection from the 7
At the next 7 · any turn

More than 7 resources: you lose none. 7 or fewer: take 1 resource of your choice, before the Klingons move.

After use: return it or flip it.

A3
Spock
RohstoffausgleichResource compensation
After any roll except a 7

If the roll gives you no resources, take 1 resource card of your choice.

After use: return it or flip it.

B6
Leonard McCoy
Entwicklungskarte aussuchenChoose a development card
Your turn · buying a development card

Replace 1 of the 3 resources with any other resource. Pick your card from the top 3 of the pile, then shuffle the pile.

After use: return it.

A2
Montgomery Scott
Hilfe beim RaumschiffbauHelp with starship building
Your turn · building a starship

Replace 1 dilithium or 1 tritanium with 1 resource of your choice.

After use: return it or flip it.

B4
Hikaru Sulu
Raumschiff versetzenRelocate a starship
Your turn

Remove 1 of your starships at the end of a route and place it somewhere else, following the normal building rules.

After use: return it.

A1
Uhura
ZwangshandelForced trade
Your turn

Demand 1 specific resource from up to 2 players. For each one you receive, give that player 1 resource of your choice back.

After use: return it or flip it.

B
Pavel Chekov
Klingonen ins AsteroidenfeldKlingons to the asteroid field
Your turn · also before the production roll

Move the Klingon cruiser to the asteroid field. Take 1 resource from the planet it left.

After use: return it.

B
Christine Chapel
Rohstoff für ArmeResources for the poor
Your turn · after the production roll

Pick 1 resource card from the hand of a player with more victory points than you.

After use: return it.

A
Janice Rand
Freier GrenzpostenFree trading post
Your turn

Choose 1 resource type. This turn, trade it 2 : 1 as often as you like.

After use: return it or flip it.

B
Sarek
Schwerter zu PflugscharenSwords to plowshares
Your turn

Discard 1 of your Sternenflotte greift ein cards, then either build an outpost for 1 tritanium + 1 dilithium, or upgrade an outpost for 2 water + 1 oxygen.

After use: return it.

Starfleet Cards

The development cards. Same deck as regular Catan (14 / 6 / 5). Look for the silver title plate at the top.

Sternenflotte greift ein
Knight · 14 cards

Starfleet IntervenesMove the Klingons to another planet and steal 1 random resource from a player with an outpost there. Keep it face up: it counts toward Largest Starfleet.

Fortschritt
Progress · 6 cards, 2 of each
  • Utopia PlanitiaRoad Building Build 2 starships for free.
  • ForschungsstationYear of Plenty Take any 2 resource cards from the supply.
  • MonopolMonopoly Name a resource. Every player gives you all their cards of it.
